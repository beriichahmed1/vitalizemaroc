## Brand

- Arabic display name: **فيتالايز المغرب**
- English display name: **Vitalize Maroc**
- Brand URL: **vitalizemaroc.shop**
- Brand scope: a curated multi-product Moroccan direct-to-consumer shop; do not position it as an energy-only brand.
- Suggested brand promise: **منتجات مختارة بعناية، وتجربة شراء واضحة حتى لباب دارك.**
- Suggested English descriptor: **Thoughtfully selected products, delivered with a clear and convenient buying experience.**

Brand promise is about the shopping experience, not a medical or product outcome. Do not call the shop a manufacturer, laboratory, clinic, importer, or product owner unless the business can substantiate that representation. A branded storefront must not imply that Vitalize manufactures or certifies supplier goods.

## ICP and emotional language

### Working ICP

Moroccan adults who discover products through TikTok, Snapchat, and other social video, want a simple way to order, and value clear product explanations, credible proof, transparent pricing, and COD.

Treat age, income, gender, health concerns, purchase intent, and platform usage as hypotheses until validated from first-party data. Do not make health conditions or sensitive-trait inferences for ad targeting.

### Communication principles

- Start with a relatable use case or frustration, not an exaggerated fear.
- Show the product, its real use, what is known, and who should not use it when the manufacturer supplies that information.
- Use Darija naturally and respectfully, without shaming, fake doctor endorsements, or pressure.
- Answer practical objections: what's in the pack, how to use it, price, delivery process, COD, and how to get help.
- Let real demonstrations and verifiable documentation carry the premium price. High price alone is not proof of quality.
- Use "معلومات المنتج" or "التفاصيل" rather than absolute health promises.

## Brand voice

Confident, warm, direct, locally fluent, and transparent. Avoid:

- "مضمون 100%" / "نتائج مضمونة" / "الأفضل في المغرب" unless legally reviewed and independently substantiated.
- Guaranteed medical, fitness, immunity, weight-loss, or performance outcomes.
- Fake urgency ("باقي غير 3") unless connected to accurate, live inventory.
- Invented testimonials, review stars, customer counts, expert quotes, certifications, or press mentions.
- Before/after claims without genuine, authorized, representative evidence and legal review.
